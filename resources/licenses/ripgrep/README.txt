Ave packages the ripgrep (rg) binary from the @vscode/ripgrep 1.18.0
platform packages (https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-ripgrep).

- ripgrep 15.0.0 (https://github.com/BurntSushi/ripgrep) is dual licensed
  under the Unlicense or MIT (see COPYING). Ave redistributes it under the
  MIT option and includes both notices: LICENSE-MIT, UNLICENSE.
- The binary is built with the pcre2 feature and statically links PCRE2
  10.45 (https://github.com/PCRE2Project/pcre2), distributed under the BSD
  licence with the PCRE2 exception: PCRE2-LICENCE.txt (upstream LICENCE.md).
- The @vscode/ripgrep wrapper and platform packages are MIT licensed:
  VSCODE-RIPGREP-LICENSE.
